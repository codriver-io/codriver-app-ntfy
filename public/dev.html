ntfy for codriver — dev harness

Fakes the codriver host: posts the same context message the driver app sends, into the same sandboxed iframe. Settings are remembered in this page's localStorage, not the widget's.

“Publish a test message” POSTs to the topic from this page, so you can watch a real message arrive over the real SSE stream. Equivalent to curl -d "test" ntfy.sh/<topic>.
